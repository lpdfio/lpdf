# Illustrations

Twelve colour plates by Arthur Rackham from the 1907 edition of Alice's Adventures in
Wonderland, one to open each chapter. The book was published in 1907 and the artist died in 1939,
so the plates are in the public domain in the United States. Check the status in your own country
before you publish this anywhere else.

Chapter I has no plate of its own in this edition, so it opens with the frontispiece, Alice.
Chapter VI has two plates; the one with the kitchen was used.
